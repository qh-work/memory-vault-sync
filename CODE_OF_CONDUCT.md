# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and safety concerns directly.
- Protect other people's privacy and never post private memory or credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, security, and project architecture.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate privacy
violations, credential disclosure, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. For a private report, use the repository Security tab and open a
private GitHub security advisory. Do not include private evidence in a public
issue.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to public representation of the
project.
